Things you can say

  • "Record a beat": four clicks count you in, then beatbox for the set number of bars
  • "Play it" / "Loop it 4 times" / "Stop"
  • "Make it techno": also trap, 808, house, lo-fi, rock, real or acoustic
  • "Tempo 120" / "Faster" / "Slower" / "Four bars"
  • "Add hi-hats" / "Remove snares" / "Double it" / "Add swing"
  • "Undo" / "Clear" / "Click off"
  • "Teach kick" (also snare, hi-hat): do that sound four times so it learns your mouth
  • "Calibrate": say "ta" on each click for 2 bars to fix timing

Backup buttons

Beatbox sounds: "b" / "boom" for kick, "k" / "pf" for snare, "ts" for hi-hat. Wrong drums? Say "teach kick", "teach snare" and "teach hi-hat" once each.
