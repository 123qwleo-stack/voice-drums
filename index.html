Things you can say

  • "Record a beat": after the beep, freestyle. Go quiet for 2 seconds to finish. It splits your beat into Sound 1, Sound 2… and plays it back with your own mouth sounds.
  • "Sound 1 is a kick" / "Make sound 2 a deep boomy 808 kick" / "Sound 3 is a crunchy snare with reverb"
  • Drums it knows: kick, snare, clap, hi-hat, open hat, tom, rimshot, crash, cowbell, shaker
  • Shaping words: deep, high, tight, long, punchy, crunchy, soft, bright, reverb, dry, 808, techno, real
  • "Make the kick deeper" / "Sound 2 back to my voice" / "Play sound 2"
  • "Mute sound 3" / "Sound 1 louder" / "Merge sound 3 into sound 1" (if it split one sound in two)
  • "Make them drums": guesses a drum for every sound
  • "Make it techno": also trap, house, lo-fi, real, for every drum at once
  • "Play" / "Loop it 8 times" / "Loop forever" / "Stop" / "Undo"
  • "Make it 4 bars" / "Half time" / "Double time" / "Tempo 120" / "Add swing"
