Things you can say

Recording

  • "Record part 1": beatbox, hum or whistle after the beep. It figures out which, plus the tempo. Go quiet for 2 seconds to finish.
  • "Record part 2": four clicks, then part 1 plays while you record over it
  • "Record part 2 alone": just a click to keep time, nothing else playing
  • "Record part 3 with part 1" / "Record a melody" / "Record drums" / "Record part 2 for 4 bars"
  • "Redo part 2" / "Delete part 3"

Listening

  • "Play" (everything) / "Play part 2" (just that one) / "Play part 1 and 2" / "Loop forever" / "Stop"
  • "Mute part 1" / "Unmute part 1" / "Part 2 louder" / "Part 2 is late" (nudges it)
  • "Go to part 1": opens a part to change it

Drum parts

  • "Sound 1 is a kick" / "Make sound 2 a deep boomy 808 kick" / "Make the snare crunchy" / "Make them drums"
  • "Mute sound 3" / "Merge sound 3 into sound 1" / "Sound 2 back to my voice" / "Make it techno"

Melody parts

  • "Make it a guitar": also piano, flute, strings, synth, bass, organ, marimba, bells, trumpet, sax, pad, choir, whistle
  • "Part 2 is an electric guitar" / "Octave up" / "Make it short" / "More reverb" / "Back to my voice"
  • "Turn off snap": keeps your exact notes instead of fixing them to the key

Everything

  • "Make it 4 bars" / "Tempo 120" / "Half time" / "Add swing" / "Undo" / "Calibrate"

Backup buttons

Tap a part to open it, and tap a sound card to hear it.

Headphones matter when recording over playback. Otherwise the mic records the speaker too.

If parts you record over playback land late, say "calibrate" and say "ta" on each click. Bluetooth headphones especially need this.

If voice stops working, tap anywhere on the page to wake it.
